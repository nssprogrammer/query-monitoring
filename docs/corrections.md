# Corrections made during the study

Errors we found in our own experiments or claims, and what we did about each. The paper reports only the corrected results; this list accompanies the paper and the [prediction log](prediction_log.md). The pairing correction is also stated in §2 of the paper because it affects the development-week results from Step 22 to Step 32.

- **Padding construction.** In Steps 12–13 the padded campaign assigned natural prompts to separate colluder accounts, so half the colluders ran a smaller pure campaign. Step 14 mixed padding into every account; the two-scale test's robustness to padding (0.62) holds only for the segregated construction (0.21 when mixed).
- **Sharding.** The first sharding test was unpaired and, with 1–5 shards per week, uninformative; it returned identical numbers for natural and generated campaigns. The paired 4-week version replaced it.
- **Mechanism claims.** The two-scale test was introduced as a deduplication detector; two ablations rejected that mechanism, and the claim was withdrawn.
- **Upper-edge scaling.** An extrapolation that concentrated campaigns need only N ≳ √A was not borne out under structured traffic (10 of 24 cells) and is not claimed.
- **Claims about mimicry.** An earlier draft said mimicry "evaded" detection; weekly detection fell fourfold but stayed far above the false-alarm rate, and the distillation comparison was taken where gains had saturated. Both claims were narrowed.
- **A mislabelled source in Step 32.** Step 32's notebook omitted the cell that defines the tilted campaign, so its source labelled "tilted" fell through to the padding construction with the same random seed and reproduced the padding results exactly. That row was discarded; the tilted results reported are Step 27's, and the source was removed from the rerun.
- **Query-cluster calibration and the population protocol.** The first query-cluster test standardised each topic cell with statistics from too few calibration windows, and the first realistic-share populations used a single calibration period without lagged adaptation; both produced uninterpretable false-alarm rates. Corrected versions (robust, count-adjusted cell statistics; rolling, lagged population calibration) were run and are the ones reported in the main text.
- **Pairing.** From Step 22 on, the paired null for a campaign window was another null window of the same week rather than the identical window without the campaign, as our protocol specified. With realised false-alarm rates of about 1% or less for the calibrated detectors, this changes paired detection by at most about that much; Step 35 restores identical-window pairing.
- **Uncertainty for zero false alarms.** Earlier drafts stated a 95% upper bound of about 0.3% for no false alarm in 960 windows, treating windows as independent. With the 24 test weeks as the unit the bound is about 12%; all such statements were corrected.
- **Capability before first detection.** An earlier draft of Table 2 compared the two-scale test under an anytime-valid martingale with hollowed selection under the first weekly alarm, and concluded that hollowed selection turns the frontier into a defence. Under the same rule the two detectors differ by about one week on padded campaigns; the claim was withdrawn and the table recomputed under both rules.
- **Two monitors compared as one.** An earlier draft compared the account-type capacity of Corollary 1 (a monitor that does not know the colluding accounts) with the 51 excess queries of Proposition 4 (a monitor that does) as if they bounded the same monitor. The comparison was removed.
- **Sequential prediction.** An earlier draft said mimicry multiplied anytime-valid detection time "fivefold, as Proposition~3 predicts"; the prediction is a factor of about 18, and the observed factor is limited by the conformal floor.
- **Baseline protocol.** We confirmed that MMD shared the lagged references and window-resampled calibration of every other detector.
